# 0.27.10 — Handle background Codex restarts during account switching

- Manual one-click switches stop Codex/CLI sessions again after account validation and before writing the new credentials.
- Shutdown now also stops matching Codex processes started by background supervisors during the shutdown window; supervisors themselves are left running.
- Failed shutdown still preserves the active credentials. Automatic account handoffs retain their stricter refusal of newly appeared sessions.
- Shared behavior applies to macOS and Windows.
